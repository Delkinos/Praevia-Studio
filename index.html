About

Praevia - Latin for forsight, a going-before - is the name I gave this studio when I started it in 2026. because most of what I make starts as preview: a sketch of a site before it is built, a room plan before it is furnished, a draft before the a post is right.

I work alone, by choice, across two kinds of desing that keep teaching each other things: website design and interior design. Both ask the same question: how does a person move through a space?, and what should they notice first?

I also write about the process of making things, and the ideas that come up along the way. I write the Journal above - my journey as a self-taught designer, plus picks and guides for the things I find useful, interesting, or inspiring. I hope it helps you too.
