Where the beans come from

Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia

Washed, high-altitude lots with the floral, tea-like brightness the region is known for. Our house pour-over.

Huila, Colombia

Caturra and Castillo varietals grown between 1,600–1,900m. Balanced body, caramel sweetness, low acidity.

Mandheling, Sumatra

Wet-hulled processing gives this lot its signature earthy, syrupy weight — the backbone of our dark roast blend.
